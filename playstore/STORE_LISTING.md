## Full description

```
Turn rough ideas, notes, photos, and files into finished AI drafts you can use and continue.

Prompt Lab quietly prepares the right prompt, runs it with AI, and shows you the finished result first. The prompt stays available in secondary tools when you want to inspect or refine it.

FROM ROUGH INPUT TO A FINISHED AI DRAFT
• Describe the result you want in everyday language
• Add images, screenshots, and supported documents for context
• Get the finished output without copying a prompt into another app

PROMPT TOOLS WHEN YOU NEED THEM
• Reveal the generated prompt only when you want to inspect it
• Refine it for clarity, detail, or impact
• Compare versions and review the automated readiness estimate

SAVE AND CONTINUE YOUR BEST WORK
• Start with practical examples
• Save finished outputs to your history
• Reopen, copy, export, or report content from one workspace

TRY IT BEFORE YOU SIGN UP
Your first few results are free and need no account. Create a free account to keep going, sync your history across devices, and manage your plan.

Prompt Lab is designed for creators, students, marketers, developers, and anyone who wants useful AI work without manually rewriting and moving prompts between tools.

Results are generated by AI and should be checked before you use or send them.
```
