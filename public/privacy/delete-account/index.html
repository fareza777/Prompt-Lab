Delete your AI Work Studio data

AI Work Studio · App: AI Work Studio (app.promptlab.twa) · Contact: support@prompt-lab.xyz

AI Work Studio no longer has user accounts or sign-in. Results, templates, and settings are stored only on your device, and content you submit is not kept on our servers after your result is produced.

Delete data on your device

  1. Android app: Settings → Apps → AI Work Studio → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  2. Browser: clear site data for prompt-lab.xyz in your browser settings.

Accounts created before accounts were retired

If you created an account in an earlier version, you can ask us to delete that account and all associated data (email, profile, usage records):

  1. Email support@prompt-lab.xyz from the same email address used for the account.
  2. Use subject line: AI Work Studio account deletion.
  3. We will process the request within 30 days (usually sooner).

Data that may be kept

  • Short-lived service logs and rate-limit counters required for security and abuse prevention.
  • Any earlier Google Play purchase records, which Google manages under its own policies.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy or contact support@prompt-lab.xyz.
