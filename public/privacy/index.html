Privacy Policy

AI Work Studio · Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Contact: support@prompt-lab.xyz

AI Work Studio (“we”, “our”, “the app”) helps you turn notes, photos, files, and requests into finished work documents. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

AI Work Studio has no user accounts, logins, or paid plans. We do not ask for your name or email to use the app.

  • Content you provide: requests, templates, and files you upload (images and documents), sent to our server only to produce the result you asked for. We do not store them in a database.
  • Data on your device: saved results, templates, and settings stay in your browser or app storage.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request metadata, used for rate limiting, security, and short-lived service logs.
  • Advertising (Android app): Google AdMob shows a banner and may collect device identifiers under Google’s policies.

2. How we use information

  • Create, improve, compare, and export finished work.
  • Send internal processing instructions and attachments to AI providers only to fulfill your request.
  • Prevent abuse (per-IP rate limits) and keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Review content reports you send about AI output.

3. Third-party services

We use trusted processors, including:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, API delivery, and service logs.
  • Upstash — short-lived rate-limit counters keyed by IP address.
  • AI providers — model inference (e.g. OpenRouter).
  • Google AdMob — ads in the Android app.

These providers process data according to their own policies and our instructions.

4. Data retention

Content you submit is processed in memory and not kept after the response. Rate-limit counters expire within minutes; service logs are kept for a short period. Data on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app.

5. Security

We use HTTPS and keep API keys for AI providers in server environment variables, never in the app.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access to or deletion of personal data by contacting us. See Delete your data for details, including data from accounts created before accounts were retired.

7. Children

AI Work Studio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

Questions: support@prompt-lab.xyz
