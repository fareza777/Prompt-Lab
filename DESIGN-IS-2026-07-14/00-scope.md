# PromptLab audit scope

- Audited artifact: live Google Play listing for `app.promptlab.twa`, production PWA/TWA at `https://prompt-lab.xyz`, current repository at `E:\apps\promptlab`, and the six current local Play Store marketing frames.
- Primary users: creators, students, marketers, and developers who use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or creative AI tools.
- Primary task: turn a rough idea or attached context into a structured prompt, improve or compare it, then save and reuse it.
- Constraints: the app is already live; preserve the PromptLab brand; React 19 + Vite PWA inside a Bubblewrap TWA; Google Play billing; Supabase account/quota sync; production changes must remain backward compatible.
- Accessibility floor: WCAG 2.2 AA for web UI plus TalkBack, font scaling, and external-keyboard usability in the Android TWA.
- Reference scope: Rams' ten principles, the live Play listing, current release tests, current Android lint report, and current store media. No competitor benchmark or signed-in purchase was executed.
